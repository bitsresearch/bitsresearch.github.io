Finding Private Accommodation in Falmouth & Penryn: A Neurodivergent Guide

Looking for private student accommodation in Falmouth or Penryn? This guide covers where to search, what to check at viewings and what to confirm before you sign.

By Charlie Tak Hei Kwong 鄺德希 · 14 August 2026

Finding somewhere to live in Falmouth or Penryn can get stressful quickly. One minute you are browsing rooms. The next, you have ten tabs open, someone wants a guarantor, and a housemate is asking whether you can sign today.

If you are neurodivergent, disabled or have special educational needs and disabilities (SEND), the practical details can matter a lot: noise, who you live with, the journey to campus, bills, shared spaces and whether you have somewhere to properly switch off.

So, where should you look?

A quick note on links: We’ve included external websites and groups that might be useful. They’re independent from this website, so a link does not mean we endorse them. Check the details carefully before making any payments or commitments.

1. Start with Cornwall Studentpad

Cornwall Studentpad - https://www.cornwallstudentpad.co.uk/ is the official accommodation search platform for students at Falmouth University and the University of Exeter’s Cornwall campuses.

It includes student houses, individual rooms, flats and lodgings in Falmouth, Penryn and nearby areas.

If you are moving alone, FX Plus also provides support with finding housemates. Studentpad is a sensible first stop before opening several different property sites.

2. Check local Facebook groups

Facebook groups can turn up rooms that never reach the bigger property websites.

Useful groups include:

  • Falmouth Exeter Plus - Housemate Finder
  • Falmouth Private Student Accommodation
  • Falmouth & Penryn Student Accommodation: Find Housing & Flatmates
  • Private Accommodation to Rent in Cornwall
  • Houses to Rent in Cornwall
  • Need room to rent in Falmouth
  • Falmouth & Penryn Homeswaps
  • Falmouth/Penryn Accommodation & Spare Rooms

If you are joining an existing house, use the group to find out a little more before arranging a viewing. Ask whether the house is generally quiet or social, how often people have friends over, and how cleaning and shared spaces tend to work.

For informal listings, check who you are dealing with and verify the property and tenancy details before sending money.

3. Use Rightmove and other property sites

Rightmove - https://www.rightmove.co.uk and OnTheMarket - https://www.onthemarket.com/ are useful for rooms, flats and whole student houses.

Set your budget, move-in date, preferred area and furnished or unfurnished preference before you start scrolling. If you are searching alone, filter for individual rooms. It saves you from repeatedly opening five- or six-bedroom houses that need a complete group.

4. Consider private student halls

Private purpose-built student accommodation is another option. The Quarry and Carrick View, for example, offer en-suite rooms and studios.

Bills and Wi-Fi may be included, which can reduce the number of separate payments to manage each month. Compare that with the cost, location and amount of shared space before deciding.

Before you book a viewing

Pick three things that would make the biggest difference to everyday life.

That might be a quieter bedroom, fewer housemates, an en-suite bathroom, bills included, a straightforward journey to campus, or enough kitchen space for your usual routine.

Then check those things properly at the viewing.

Listen for traffic and noise from communal areas. Look at the bedroom light and storage. Check how many people share the kitchen and bathroom, and map the actual journey to campus rather than relying on the description in the advert.

Before you sign anything

The housing market can move quickly, but urgency is not a reason to sign a contract you do not understand.

Check:

  • total rent and deposit
  • tenancy dates
  • whether bills are included
  • guarantor requirements
  • who deals with repairs
  • whether the tenancy is joint or individual

If something is unclear, ask before signing or paying anything.

Quick FAQ

Can I find a room if I do not already have housemates?

Yes. Look for individual rooms in existing student houses, lodgings or studio accommodation. FX Plus also provides support with finding housemates.

Is Penryn too far from Falmouth University?

It depends on your campus and how you travel. Check the actual walking, bus or train journey from the property before deciding.

What if I need quieter accommodation?

Look beyond the word “quiet” in the advert. Check the road, bedroom position, number of housemates and distance from kitchens or shared areas. If your requirements relate to a disability or health condition, you can also speak with university accommodation or disability support services.

Before committing, check the property against the few things that matter most to your day-to-day routine. A room can look good online and still be the wrong setup once you are living there.
