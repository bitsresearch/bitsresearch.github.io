What Kind of Storyteller Are You?

Choose what feels most like you. Then try a tiny story prompt — in words, pictures, sound, collage or simply in your head.

By Charlie Tak Hei Kwong 鄺德希 · 1 September 2026

This is for fun and reflection — not a test or a label. Pick the answer that feels right today.

You can write, draw, make a collage, record a voice note — or just think. No art skills or sharing needed.

Where did these ideas come from?

The quiz is a playful way into four different approaches to storytelling. These are not fixed or proven personality types. They draw on BITS research about stories, belonging, disability and creative expression.
