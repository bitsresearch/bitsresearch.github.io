Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Join

Who can take part?

BITS workshops are open to students aged 18 or over who are in their first or foundation year of university.

Do I need SEND or a diagnosis to join?

No. You do not need a formal diagnosis, and students without SEND can register too.

Do I need to be good at art?

Not at all. You do not need any art or creative experience.

What to Expect

What actually happens in a workshop?

You will try a short creative activity using approaches such as words, images, colours, symbols or simple digital storytelling.

Will I have to speak in front of everyone?

No. You choose how much you want to say or share.

Access & Comfort

Can I take a break or leave early?

Yes. You can pause, take a break or leave whenever you need to.

Can I ask for accessibility support?

Yes. You can let Charlie know what would make the workshop easier or more comfortable without disclosing a diagnosis.

Research & Privacy

Is this a research study?

Yes. BITS is a doctoral research project led by Charlie Kwong at Falmouth University.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. Taking part is voluntary, and you can choose to withdraw.
